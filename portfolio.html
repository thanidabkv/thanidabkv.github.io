Get to know me

Stories and heritage

I'm a creative from Thailand studying at Syracuse University. I grew up across Bangkok, Nakhon Si Thammarat and Chonburi, which made me realize the best experiences often come with stories and a rich heritage. When I'm not working on branding projects or rebranding my family's restaurant, I'm experimenting with new creative projects or perfecting my film photography.

From architecture to PR

Why people are drawn in

Architecture taught me how to design spaces, but what fascinates me is why people are drawn to them. My background in spatial design taught me that every element has a purpose, and that same philosophy drives how I approach branding strategy. That curiosity led me to public relations: I've interned as a PR coordinator at Vivaldi Public Relations, led brand strategy and social media for multiple brands, and founded Mi Clair Nails.

What drives me

The invisible threads

I'm endlessly curious about cultural trends, human behavior and the subtle ways people communicate who they are. I appreciate luxury, beauty and thoughtful visual storytelling, in physical spaces and digital content, and I love projects that blend aesthetics with meaning: the invisible threads that connect people to places, brands and each other.

What I love

Photography

I got into film photography because of my grandfather's Nikon FM2. Analog taught me to appreciate everyday moments, not just special occasions. From Bangkok to Syracuse, every city has stories waiting to be discovered.

Language & culture

I'm learning Korean and Chinese because I'm fascinated by how language shapes culture, and I bring those cross-cultural insights, along with Thai heritage, into my creative work.

Entrepreneurship

Founding Mi Clair Nails and Engerra taught me to balance creativity with commercial viability, manage budgets, and take ideas from concept to sales.

Drawing & illustration

I wrote and illustrated The Steps of Mindfulness, now in 300+ public libraries in Thailand, and won a Tetra Award at the MPED International Art & Design Competition for a piece on mental health in the K-pop industry.

Ikebana

The Japanese art of flower arrangement (生花, "making flowers alive") taught me to create space for beauty rather than impose it, and to value the creative journey over the outcome.

Self-discovery

I value mindfulness, personal growth and understanding myself deeply, and I enjoy helping people understand themselves and others through stories and creative work.

Fun fact I first fell in love with graphic design in 10th grade, designing a magazine for World Literature class. That class assignment became the start of everything from event posters to brand identities.
